Power Connections & Important Notes

🔋 Power Distribution

ESP32 VIN / 5VExternal 5V DC supply (USB or buck converter)
Relay Module VCC5V (JD-VCC with optocoupler jumpers)
Relay Module GNDCommon GND with ESP32
Sensors VCC3.3V (Flame, MQ-2, HC-SR04)
Buzzer / LED relaysActive-low via relay module (LOW = operate)
AC Loads (230V)Through relay COM / NO terminals only

⚠️ Relay Wiring (Active-Low)

Relay OFF stateGPIO set HIGH → relay de-energized
Relay ON stateGPIO set LOW → relay energized
Load connectionLive → COM, Appliance → NO (normally open)
SwitchesOne leg to GPIO, other to GND (pullup enabled)
HC-SR04Use voltage divider on Echo if powered at 5V
⚠️ DANGER: 230V AC mains connected to relay contacts can cause electric shock or fire if miswired. Always use a fused, isolated enclosure. Keep low-voltage sensor wiring and high-voltage AC wiring physically separated. Relay channels are active-low — the ESP32 pin must be LOW to energize the relay.
💡 Note about Light Bulbs 3 & 4: GPIO 32 and GPIO 33 are reserved for the two new relay channels (Light Bulb 3 & 4) added to the dashboard. To activate them on the physical ESP32, add the relay pin definitions and control logic to esp32/main.ino (or wire them to the free relay channels of a 6-channel relay module). They work exactly like Relays 1 & 2 — active-low outputs.
